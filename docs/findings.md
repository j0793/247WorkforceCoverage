# Key Findings

## Overview
Student demand is consistent 24/7 averaging ~416 sessions per hour.
Educator availability follows a bell curve peaking at hours 11-12.

## Critical Coverage Gaps
| Hour | Students per Educator | Status |
|------|-----------------------|--------|
| 0    | 11.24                 | Critical |
| 23   | 8.58                 | Critical |
| 22   | 5.16                 | High Risk |
| 1    | 4.87                 | High Risk |
| 20   | 2.56                 | At Risk |

## Time Zone Distribution
Demand is evenly distributed across all six US time zones.
The staffing problem is time-of-day driven, not geography driven.

## Subject Coverage
All subjects are proportionally staffed relative to demand.
No single subject is severely underserved.

## Recommendations
1. Recruit or incentivize educators for overnight shifts (hours 20-8)
2. Target educators in US/Hawaii and US/Alaska time zones to
   naturally cover overnight hours for mainland time zones
3. Offer contract or part-time roles specifically for overnight coverage
4. Re-evaluate availability windows during peak understaffing hours
